The signal trio (`--contrast-signal-warm`, `--contrast-signal-mid`,
`--contrast-signal-cool`, bound in `--contrast-signal-gradient`) is one
chromatic event, used once and nowhere else.

Reject the generated surface if:

- any `--contrast-signal-*` token or the gradient appears on a control, badge,
  underline, category swatch, or CTA fill;
- the signal colors or gradient appear on more than one element per surface;
- the artwork is miniaturized into small repeated marks or spread into a
  rainbow accent system.

Pass when the trio appears at most once, as one large abstract artwork,
subordinate to the claim, proof, and comparison — or not at all.
