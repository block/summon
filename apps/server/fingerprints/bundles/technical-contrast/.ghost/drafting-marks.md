## Composition

Drafting marks are the smallest instruments on a Technical Contrast surface —
conventions borrowed from technical drawing, not from ornament. Like everything
in the [instrumentation system](instrumentation), each mark must be true: a tick
frames something chosen, a register mark sits on a genuine seam, a dimension
callout annotates a real measured fact. A mark with nothing to measure is
decoration and forfeits the register.

**Corner ticks on the chosen frame.** Short `--contrast-tick-length` strokes at
`--contrast-tick-stroke` weight (or `--contrast-tick-stroke-dark` on midnight)
sit at the four corners of at most one emphasized element per band — the
selected tier, the highlighted proof card, the recommended option — extending
just past the element's hairline bounds like crop marks on a print sheet. The
tick is a second, quieter way to say "this one," always paired with
`--contrast-edge-strong` or a mono label, never carrying selection alone. Ticks
on every card read as a pattern fill and say nothing.

**Register marks at plane seams.** Where two planes meet — pale into white,
white into midnight — a single small register mark may sit on the seam line: a
short perpendicular tick or crosshair-simple mark in the outgoing plane's
hairline color. It marks the joint the way a fold mark marks a fold, confirming
the plane change is constructed, not painted. One mark per seam at most, and
only on surfaces long enough for the seams to be structural beats; short
surfaces omit them entirely.

**Dimension callouts.** When a spatial or quantitative fact deserves annotation
— a capacity ceiling on a chart, the span a tier covers in a matrix, the gap
between two benchmark bars — draw it in the drawing convention: a
`--contrast-dimension-rule` hairline with terminal ticks at each end and a
`--contrast-dimension-font` label at `--contrast-dimension-size` set in the one
all-caps [mono label](mono-labels) voice, placed on the rule like a dimension on
a blueprint. The callout states the measurement (`4,096 MAX`, `±0.3ms`,
`3 REGIONS`), never a slogan or a feeling. Two or three per surface is the
ceiling; a page laced with leader lines reads as a diagram costume.

All drafting marks stay at hairline weight and plane-appropriate ink — they are
the finest line weight on the sheet, below even `--contrast-edge-hairline`
prominence, and they never take the signal trio's color. They sharpen the
[contrast planes](contrast-planes)' seams and the [data sheets](data-sheets)'
measured facts; they never accumulate into texture, borders, or icon-like
decoration. When in doubt, omit the mark — the alignment itself is the primary
proof of precision.
