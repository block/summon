description: "Guard against source-brand leakage — not real platform names, logos, model names, or exact pricing; instead prompt-named fictional identity with composition-only heritage; recognize the switch by any proper noun or figure traceable to a real vendor. Reach before naming anything on a generated surface."
---

**Not** brand reuse. Technical Contrast descends from public technical-platform
research, and everything recognizable from that research is off-limits as
content: a real platform, company, or product name, domain, slogan, wordmark,
or logo; real customer logos, conference marks, or recognition badges used as
proof; real model names, exact source pricing values, or verbatim source
headlines and copy; proprietary screenshots or copied hero/signal artwork;
terminal-brand gestures (a source's stencil sign-off, wordmark lockup, or
tagline) reproduced literally.

**Instead** the heritage travels as composition only — contrast planes, white
data sheets, midnight proof planes, mono eyebrows, pricing-sheet anatomy,
proof-card anatomy, signal-artwork *placement*. Identity content is fictional,
user-supplied, or derived from the user's prompt:

- name the product, models, and tiers from the prompt, never from the source
  inspiration;
- use generic placeholders for customer logos, conference marks, and pricing
  values unless the prompt supplies them;
- render the constant anchor lockup (`--contrast-anchor-*`) and the metadata
  strip with prompt-derived names;
- translate any terminal-brand gesture into a generic low-contrast sign-off
  tinted near the hairline surface color, or omit it.

**Recognize the switch by** any proper noun, domain, logo mark, model
identifier, or price figure that a reader could trace to a real vendor. If a
web search for the string would land on a real company, it does not belong on
the sheet.
