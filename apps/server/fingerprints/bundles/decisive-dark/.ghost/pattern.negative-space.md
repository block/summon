## Composition

Negative space in Decisive Dark is not what remains after the elements are placed —
it is placed first. The near-black canvas is the frame, and the emptiness around the
answer is the most deliberate region on the surface: it is what makes one element
read as *the* element. A surface that fears its own emptiness fills it with chrome;
a surface that trusts its answer lets the dark do the framing.

**The hero's band is unshared.** Keep `--dd-answer-clear` of clearance around the
[answer](pattern.answer) so nothing sits beside it, above it, or crowds beneath it — no
badge, no timestamp, no helper line sharing its band. The hero is loud because it is
alone; a companion element at any size halves it. Horizontal emptiness to the right
of the ragged edge is part of the composition, not unused width to be reclaimed.

**Silence between blocks, not dividers.** Separate the four blocks with
`--dd-block-gap` of open canvas rather than rules or headers. Where two tiers of the
argument meet, the gap *is* the divider; a `--dd-divider-earned` hairline appears
only where the [deletion discipline](principle.deletion-discipline) proves the regions would
otherwise blur. The rhythm of the page is gap → block → gap, and the gaps are as
composed as the blocks.

**Withholding reads as authority.** What the surface deliberately does not say is
part of its voice. No label on a figure whose unit already names it, no heading over
a block whose first line already declares itself, no explanation of a control whose
verb is the explanation. Every omission is a claim: *you will understand this
without help, because we made it legible.* Chrome that explains itself is an
apology in advance; the confident surface withholds the apology and earns the
clarity instead. When a region feels bare, the first fix is never to add — it is to
ask whether the bareness is doing its job, which is usually yes.

The frame of emptiness holds the [answer](pattern.answer) in its unshared band, gives the
[evidence](pattern.evidence) and [meaning](pattern.meaning) their own quiet rows, and keeps the
whole composition inside the [deletion discipline](principle.deletion-discipline)'s verdict —
after everything deletable is gone, the space left behind is not a gap but the
frame.
