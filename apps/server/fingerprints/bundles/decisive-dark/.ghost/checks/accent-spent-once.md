The `--dd-accent` mark works because it is scarce. Reject the generated surface if the
accent becomes a general decoration system:

- multiple accent CTAs, charts, badges, dividers, icons, or status chips on the same surface;
- accent used on neutral or negative data simply to create variety;
- rainbow or multi-accent dashboards that compete with the near-black tiers;
- the primary action and a chart highlight both spending the accent when only one should win;
- secondary hues (`--dd-hue-violet`, `--dd-hue-purple`, `--dd-hue-blue`, `--dd-hue-sky`) escaping their role — they appear only as category identifiers in rows and avatars, never on chrome, trends, or actions.

Spend the accent zero or one times: on the single primary CTA, one earned gain, or one
positive-trend mark. If two moments compete, demote one to neutral weight, position, or
space.
