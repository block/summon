Meaning is required on every Decisive Dark surface: one calm, declarative sentence
in `--color-text-subtle` with the load-bearing value lifted to
`--color-text-standard`. Reject the generated surface if:

- the meaning is missing, or the reader is left to interpret the data themselves;
- the meaning runs to a paragraph — one sentence, not three;
- the copy hedges (*"you might want to…"*), apologizes, or takes a customer-service tone instead of naming the pattern, buffer, or downside directly;
- the meaning restates the figure the reader just saw, or softens its first clause with a second;
- the surface breaks its numeric floors — less than `--dd-top-safe` (56px) of top breathing room for host chrome, or body copy below `--text-sm`.

The sentence says what the data means for this person and stops.
