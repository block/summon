Decisive Dark gives the answer before the reader studies the page. Reject the generated
surface if the first read is not a single dominant verdict or metric:

- four equal KPI cards, a balanced dashboard, or a neutral overview grid fighting for attention;
- centered marketing-hero posture instead of a left-aligned answer with a leading element;
- redundant page titles, tooltips, captions, or decorative chrome delaying the useful answer;
- hero type that is clipped, crowded, or shares its band with competing elements.

The hero answer must be the loudest region on first paint: display-sized numeral or
verdict headline, sufficient `--dd-answer-clear`, and everything else demoted to support.
