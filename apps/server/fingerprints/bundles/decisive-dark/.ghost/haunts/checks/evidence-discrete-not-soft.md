Decisive Dark proves the answer with evidence that reads in one glance. Reject the
generated surface if supporting data becomes soft or ornamental:

- smoothed line charts, decorative waves, vague blobs, or gradient data art replacing bars/candles;
- orphaned stats without axis labels, units, or an interpreting so-what;
- dense multicolor chart palettes that make every series equal emphasis;
- evidence that arrives after long prose instead of directly supporting the answer.

Use discrete `--dd-bar` bars, candlesticks, or tight uniform rows on near-black tiers.
Pair the evidence with the required one-sentence meaning so the reader knows what it
means without reading a paragraph.
