## Not this

Decisive Dark refuses the generic generated dashboard where nothing has won:

- **Not** four equal-weight KPI cards, a balanced overview grid, or a neutral
  dashboard of modules fighting for attention. **Instead** one governing
  `--dd-numeral` metric or verdict headline at `--dd-hero-size`, with every
  other figure demoted into the [evidence](pattern.evidence) region beneath it.
- **Not** a centered marketing-hero splash on a functional surface. **Instead**
  a left-aligned answer with a leading element and a clean ragged edge — the
  emptiness right of the rag is composition, not unused width.
- **Not** a badge, timestamp, helper line, or redundant page title sharing the
  hero's band. **Instead** `--dd-answer-clear` of unshared clearance — a
  display numeral with a companion leaning on it is half as loud as one
  standing alone.
- **Not** a second spend of `--dd-accent` — an accent CTA *and* an accent
  chart stroke, accent badges, accent dividers, or accent used on neutral data
  for variety. **Instead** `--dd-accent-budget: 1`: pick the moment that
  carries the most meaning and demote the loser to neutral weight, position,
  or space. Zero is correct when the answer is steady.

## Recognize the switch

You have slid into competing heroes the moment any of these is true: two
elements could each plausibly be "the answer"; the eye has to choose where to
start; `--dd-accent` (`#ffb020`) appears at two coordinates; the first region
is centered; or the hero's band contains anything but the hero. The test is
one second on first paint — if the takeaway is not already read, the screen
has not made a choice, and the fix is always demotion or deletion, never
adding emphasis to the challenger.

The replacement discipline lives in the [answer block](pattern.answer), the
[accent moment](pattern.accent-moment), and the [negative-space
frame](pattern.negative-space); the pass that enforces it is
[deletion discipline](principle.deletion-discipline).
