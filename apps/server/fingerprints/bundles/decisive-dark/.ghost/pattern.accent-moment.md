## Composition

The accent is not a color used for decoration — it is the mark of action, the single
most recognizable signal in the system, and Decisive Dark spends it with discipline.
The rule is `--dd-accent-budget`: zero or one accent moment per surface, and never
two. Where the accent appears, something is about to happen; every extra accent
dilutes the signal.

**One earned moment.** Spend `--dd-accent` on exactly one of these per surface: a
positive-trend stroke or the top of a winning [evidence](pattern.evidence) bar, an earned gain
or "+" amount in a row, or the one primary CTA in the [action stack](pattern.action) where the
action itself is the accent moment. Pick the moment that carries the most meaning and
let everything else stay neutral — text in `--color-text-standard` and
`--color-text-subtle`, surfaces in the near-black tiers, actions as neutral `--dd-pill`
pills.

**When zero is right.** Zero accent is correct, not a gap, when the answer is steady,
reassuring, or has no clear winner — a figure that is simply fine, a summary with no
victor, a neutral status. Do not force the accent to fill the rule; ask whether the
moment carries it. The rule is *never more than one*, not *always at least one*.

**Scarcity is the design, not a constraint on it.** The accent works because
everything around it withholds color: the near-black tiers and neutral ink are
what make one warm stroke legible from across the room. Treat the budget the way
the whole surface treats every element — as a cost paid only for a clear reason.
An accent that must compete with a second accent, a colorful badge, or a mood
tint has already lost its meaning; the discipline that deletes the extra label is
the same discipline that keeps the accent singular. When the accent does appear,
it appears settled — no pulse, no glow, no attention animation. Its stillness is
the point: the color says *act here* once, plainly, and trusts that once is
enough.

The accent moment is the surface's one flash of action color; it lives in the
[answer](pattern.answer) when a metric trends up, in the [evidence](pattern.evidence) when a chart
turns positive, or in the [action stack](pattern.action) when the CTA is primary — but only
ever in one of them.

**Bound vs open.** Bound: `--dd-accent-budget` of one — zero or one accent
moment per surface, never two; the accent marks action, gain, or positive trend
only, never mood or decoration; where it appears, it appears settled — no pulse,
no glow, no attention animation. Open: which of the three homes it lands in
(answer trend, winning evidence bar, or primary CTA), and whether it appears at
all — zero is correct when the answer is steady or has no clear winner.
