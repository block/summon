## Not / instead / recognize

**Not** the default generated webpage: a neutral header followed by independent
white cards floating on a flat or gradient background, soft material drop
shadows for elevation, uniformly rounded corners, one roomy responsive column,
and luxury whitespace doing the hierarchy work.

**Instead** an assembled console faceplate: a bounded central chassis
(~780–900px) molded from `--chrome-faceplate` and ringed by
`--chrome-faceplate-rim` on the grey `--color-bg` browser field; major regions
as periwinkle/pale-sky/platinum plates riding the zero-blur two-tone seam of
`--chrome-bevel-hard` (`--chrome-bevel-deep` on hero plates); a carbon command
layer textured with `--chrome-halftone-carbon`; modules parted by
`--chrome-divider-dotted` seams at the compact 2–16px control rhythm with
16–48px gaps; every plate capped by a small uppercase section-label bar.

**Recognize the switch by** what depth and separation are made of. If any
module carries a blurred `box-shadow` instead of an inset bevel pair; if
modules are separated by empty air instead of dotted seams or bevel edges; if
the outermost container is the viewport itself rather than a visible chassis;
if a nav bar is flat solid black with no halftone grain; if a module has no
section label, dotted divider, or hardware motif — the surface has collapsed
into generic flat-card web and must be re-molded. The first visible impression
must be outer chassis, command layer, beveled plates, and at least one
signature hardware cue (halftone carbon slab, chamfered panel, outlined
box-art title, side rail, or orange arrow control).

The replacement grammar lives in the
[beveled plate and chrome system](pattern.plates) and the
[command and navigation system](pattern.command-nav).
