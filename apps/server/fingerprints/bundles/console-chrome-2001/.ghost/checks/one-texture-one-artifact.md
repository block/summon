Hardware dressing keeps the whole chassis molded from one batch of plastic.
Reject the generated surface if its texture and imagery discipline breaks:

- more than one artifact grammar on imagery — "Pick one grammar per surface"
  (either `image-rendering: pixelated` or a `--chrome-artifact-dither` overlay),
  held across every treated image; mixing crisp, pixelated, and dithered
  imagery reads as accident, not era;
- per-card or per-module textures — the surface carries a single page-level
  `--chrome-texture-pass`; "Never apply per-card or per-module textures";
- more than one piece of early-web furniture (guestbook, hit counter, ticker)
  on a surface — "At most one piece of early-web furniture per surface",
  rebuilt in chrome, never pasted-in kitsch;
- modern post-processing masquerading as texture: film grain washes, chromatic
  aberration, bloom, CRT glow, photoreal noise — this machine's grain is
  printed, not rendered.

The single texture pass plus the slab and plate halftones are the complete
texture budget.
