Console Chrome 2001 is source-informed and playful, not source-branded. Reject the
generated surface if it reuses protected or recognizable identity:

- real console, game, studio, character, controller, mascot, cartridge, or platform names;
- copied logos, wordmarks, character silhouettes, button glyph arrangements, or UI screenshots;
- nav labels, ratings, product panels, or hero copy traced from an older site;
- invented lookalike mascots that clearly point back to a known property.

Use generic hardware metaphors, original mascots, speech bubbles, cartridges,
controllers, stars, arrows, circuitry, and prompt-derived naming. Public research informs
composition and texture only.
