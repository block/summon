Warm color in Console Chrome 2001 must explain interaction. Reject the generated surface
if orange, amber, or red are used as generic decoration:

- orange section backgrounds, hero washes, or passive chips with no forward/submit/open role;
- amber used as a broad brand fill instead of tools, badges, tabs, search, and utility affordances;
- rainbow category systems that weaken the chrome-and-command palette;
- red used as a page fill or routine accent instead of sparse identity/error marking.

Forward, submit, continue, open, and launch actions read as signal orange. Amber stays
for utility and badge-like controls. Steady surfaces remain cool periwinkle, pale sky,
platinum, white, and carbon.
