## Not / instead / recognize

**Not** source-branded retro: real console, game, studio, character,
controller, mascot, cartridge, or platform names; copied logos, wordmarks,
character silhouettes, button glyph arrangements, or UI screenshots; nav
labels, ratings, product panels, or hero copy traced from an older site; and
no invented lookalike mascots that clearly point back to a known property.
Rating-authority marks and regulatory badges are equally off-limits unless the
user supplies them as content to transform.

**Instead** source-agnostic playfulness built from generic machine vernacular:
original or abstract mascots and speech bubbles, cartridges, controllers,
stars, arrows, and circuitry as motifs; product and nav naming derived from the
user's prompt; fake-but-plausible hardware legends (model number CC-2001A,
AV OUT · EXT.2 port labels, a PAL/NTSC region badge) etched in `--font-mono`
silkscreen on chrome zones only. One brand-neutral mascot or playful signoff
moment per surface, small and useful, never a large illustration replacing the
console UI.

**Recognize the switch by** provenance of every proper noun and figure: if any
name, mark, silhouette, or layout on the surface could be traced to a real
console maker, game, or older site rather than to the user's prompt or this
fingerprint's own generic vernacular, it is borrowed IP and must be renamed or
redrawn. Public research informs composition and texture only — never
permission to reuse protected characters or wordmarks.

The permitted dressing vocabulary lives in the
[hardware dressing system](pattern.hardware-dressing) and the
[badge and section-label system](pattern.badges).
