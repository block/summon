Garden Notes should feel like paper on paper, not a pile of app cards. Reject the
generated surface if depth becomes generic elevation:

- cards inside cards inside panels, all with equal rounded boxes and shadows;
- heavy drop shadows, glass, or floating dashboard tiles replacing note-paper layering;
- flat white panels instead of warm `--garden-note-paper` and kept-note tones;
- multiple picked-up/floating notes at once.

Use one bounded outer rounded shell, warm cream note panels, one gentle outer lift, and
only the few layered notes that need separating. At most one note is picked up for a
closer look while the rest recedes.
