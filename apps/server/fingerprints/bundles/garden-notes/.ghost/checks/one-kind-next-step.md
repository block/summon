Garden Notes lowers pressure by choosing the next small move. Reject the generated
surface if the plan does not visibly select one kind first action:

- kanban boards, streak dashboards, or equal task cards with no primary next step;
- green emphasis (`--garden-chosen-dot`, `--color-accent` fill) on more than one element, or two equally loud buttons in one panel — green appearing twice is the recognized switch into an app;
- hard winner/loser verdicts or numeric scorecards for low-stakes personal choices;
- productivity verbs such as crush, optimize, maximize, perfect, hack, dominate, or hustle;
- a plan that requires reading every note before the first action is clear.

Highlight one good-enough recommendation or first action in a soft callout with the
green `--garden-chosen-dot`, and name the accepted tradeoff gently.
