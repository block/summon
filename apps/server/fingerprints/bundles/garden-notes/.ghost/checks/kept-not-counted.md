Done is a resting state, not an achievement to celebrate. Reject the generated
surface if completion turns congratulatory or accounted:

- strikethrough, grey-out, or vanishing rows on finished steps instead of settling onto `--garden-kept-paper` with `--garden-kept-ink`;
- a "Kept" chip with a success-green fill instead of the standard quiet `--garden-chip-face`/`--garden-chip-ink` recipe;
- completion counts, percentages, streaks, or any number celebrating throughput ("12 kept!");
- praise copy, confetti, or cheering standing in for the plan quietly holding what got done.

Let finished steps settle as kept notes — text intact, plain quiet "Kept" chip
naming the state in words. If a surface needs to summarize what got done, a
short row of kept notes says it better than a number ever could.
