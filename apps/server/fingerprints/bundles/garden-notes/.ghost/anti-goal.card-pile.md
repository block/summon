Garden Notes depth collapses the moment layering becomes generic elevation.
This guard names the pile before it forms.

**Not** cards inside cards inside panels, all with equal rounded boxes and their
own shadows. **Not** heavy drop shadows, glassmorphism, or floating dashboard
tiles standing in for note-paper layering. **Not** flat white (`#ffffff`)
panels — the language has no flat white; every surface is warm cream. **Not**
multiple notes picked up, floating, or elevated at once. **Not** hover effects
that change elevation — the resting lift is constant.

**Instead**: one bounded outer rounded shell holding the whole plan; every panel
filled with the `--garden-note-paper` gradient (falling to `#f6f0cf` at its
base) and dusted with `--garden-paper-texture`; a single shared
`--garden-note-lift` (`0 14px 34px rgba(66, 99, 58, 0.14)`) on the few notes
that genuinely need separating — the primary action, a prep basket, a can-wait
slip — with everything else flat on the panel, separated by the airy `--space-*`
ladder rather than shadow. Layered-paper feeling comes from position and shared
cream tones (`--garden-kept-paper` for settled notes), never from nesting. The
one sanctioned deeper lift is `--garden-pickup-lift` on a single held note,
only while held, with the rest of the page receding behind
`--garden-recede-veil`.

**Recognize the switch by**: a second shadow value appearing anywhere at rest;
a note that contains another shadowed note; pure white where cream belongs;
two notes elevated at the same time; or every note on the page carrying the
same weight so nothing reads as the first action. Equal-weight piles flatten
the plan and hide the next step — when in doubt, remove the shadow and add
space.
