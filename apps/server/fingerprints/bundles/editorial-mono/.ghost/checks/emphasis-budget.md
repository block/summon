Editorial Mono stays decisive because its loud moves are rationed. Reject the
generated surface if any budget is overspent:

- more than one specimen figure per evidence region — the oversized serif fact
  loses its authority and the page becomes a KPI wall wearing serif;
- revealed-measure guide rules used more than once per surface, or spread as an
  all-over overlay instead of framing the one region that carries the comparison;
- a lettered index ladder on fewer than three sequential movements — the ladder
  exists to make a real sequence legible, and fewer than three movements take
  no index characters;
- more than one verdict frame per document — the paired hairlines mark the one
  place the document commits, and framing a second region collapses the frame
  into decoration;
- less than 72px of top breathing room above the masthead — the display type
  must land after a pause, not crowd the host chrome.
