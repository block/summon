Editorial Mono earns trust through visible measure. Reject the generated surface if
facts are scattered into generic cards:

- rounded pastel cards or shadowed metric tiles replacing ruled evidence bands;
- comparison options with different criteria, labels, or internal structures;
- orphaned stats with no interpretation, implication, rank, or shared label;
- sorting/filtering that hides the catalog's everything-visible ordering.

Use hairline rules, shared columns, mono counters, aligned labels, and visible criteria.
Every fact needs a so-what, and every comparison should let the reader compare like
against like.
