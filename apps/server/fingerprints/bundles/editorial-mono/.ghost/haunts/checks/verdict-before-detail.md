Editorial Mono is organized around a claim, not a neutral inventory. Reject the generated
surface if the first major region postpones judgment:

- generic titles such as Overview, Summary, or Dashboard with no verdict attached;
- evidence tables, item grids, or paragraphs appearing before the recommendation or outcome;
- multiple competing claim panels instead of one editorial hierarchy;
- passive recap language where the page should commit to a decision, risk, or next action.

Open with the masthead/deck/folio shell or the single inverse ink panel so the verdict is
legible first. Evidence should justify the claim rather than make the reader infer it.
