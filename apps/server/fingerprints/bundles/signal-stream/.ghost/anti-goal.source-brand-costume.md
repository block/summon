## Not this

Signal Stream is informed by public editorial sources, and the failure mode is
wearing one as a costume: a generated surface that reuses a real publication
name, wordmark, or masthead; a real author or byline name; a verbatim source
headline, article title, or section label; or ad creative and sponsored-module
copy carried over as if it were content. The lineage (Bloomberg-density,
hazard-signage, poster typography) is named as heritage — it is never copied
as identity.

## Instead

The fingerprint informs **composition only**. Source evidence becomes generic
roles: lead stack, latest rail, segmented stream tabs, section digest,
saturated interruption tile, masthead *placement*. Identity content is always
one of three things — fictional, user-supplied, or a task-derived title
(Signal Brief, Launch File, Field Notes, the user's product name). A masthead
is a composition role: `--signal-shout-*` display scale plus placement, never
copied letterforms or a real mark. Ref codes come from the surface's own
invented codebook (SIG-01, FIELD/A), never a source's section taxonomy.

## Recognize the switch by

- any real publisher name or wordmark rendered on the surface, at any scale;
- a byline naming a real journalist, or a headline lifted verbatim;
- section labels that are a specific publication's taxonomy rather than
  generic roles;
- sponsored/ad copy from a source treated as editorial content.

When any of these appear, abstract: keep the composition the source suggested,
replace the identity with a fictional or task-derived one, and let the
uppercase mono metadata carry the orientation instead of the borrowed brand.
